The operating sequence

Diagnose first. Build only what matters.

  1. Week 01

    Diagnose

    Review the current offer, pipeline, sales process, proof and economics. Identify the specific point where good prospects are lost.

  2. Build

    Position and connect

    Rework the offer and message, then connect the page, acquisition channel, booking flow, CRM and follow-up.

  3. Launch

    Measure real movement

    Track qualified responses, applications, calls, proposals and wins—not vanity metrics in isolation.

  4. Improve

    Fix the weakest link

    Use the evidence to improve targeting, messaging, qualification or the close before adding more traffic.
